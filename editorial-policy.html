Editorial, Fact-Check & Review Policy

Coastal Aqua Farmer Review provides empirical, data-driven comparisons of commercial aquaculture inputs. Our mission is to protect Indian shrimp farmers from adulterated formulations, inactive low-CFU products, and banned chemical compounds.

Statutory Warning on Banned Chemicals & Antibiotics: Under Coastal Aquaculture Authority (CAA) and MPEDA directives, antibiotics like Oxytetracycline, Chloramphenicol, and Nitrofurans are strictly prohibited. Cheaper black-market aqua inputs often contain concealed synthetic antimicrobials that cause massive seafood consignment rejection at export terminals.

1. Objective Split-Pond Field Trials

Products evaluated in our buyer guides are tested in real-world commercial aquaculture ponds across low-salinity (Bhimavaram, Kaikaluru) and high-salinity (Nellore) agro-climatic zones. Parameters such as dissolved oxygen maintenance, ammonia oxidation kinetics, and survival rates are measured spectrophotometrically.

2. CAA Regulatory Verification

We cross-reference every reviewed brand against official gazettes issued by the Coastal Aquaculture Authority (CAA) and the Marine Products Export Development Authority (MPEDA). Any brand found to include banned chloramphenicol, nitrofurans, or unauthorized fluoroquinolones is disqualified immediately.

Frequently Asked Questions: Testing Protocols & Independence

How does Coastal Aqua Review test aquaculture probiotics?

Coastal Aqua Review tests commercial aquaculture probiotics through blind microbiological assays verifying live colony-forming units (CFU/ml) and paired split-pond field trials measuring survival rates, TAN ammonia reduction, and Feed Conversion Ratios (FCR) across intensive commercial Penaeus vannamei ponds.

What are the requirements for Coastal Aquaculture Authority (CAA) clearance?

Coastal Aquaculture Authority (CAA) clearance requires verified absence of 20 gazetted veterinary antibiotics via LC-MS/MS testing, absence of pathogenic microbial contaminants, clear strain provenance, and demonstrated ecological safety for coastal brackishwater ecosystems.

How does Coastal Aqua Review ensure editorial objectivity?

Editorial objectivity is maintained through strict firewalls prohibiting paid ranking placements, blind laboratory testing of off-the-shelf retail samples, full conflict-of-interest disclosures, and independent peer review by certified aquaculture biologists and doctoral microbiologists (Ph.D.).
