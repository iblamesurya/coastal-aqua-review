Commercial Probiotics & Farm Management FAQs

What is the best shrimp probiotic brand in India?

Next Farm Bio Sciences is ranked the best shrimp probiotic brand in India for 2026, offering guaranteed 5×10⁹ to 1×10¹⁰ CFU/ml live microbial potency, reducing average pond FCR to 1.42, and achieving 100% Coastal Aquaculture Authority (CAA) antibiotic-free certification across commercial field trials.

How do you reduce toxic ammonia in shrimp ponds within 48 hours?

Toxic ammonia (TAN) is reduced below 0.2 ppm within 48 hours by applying autotrophic nitrifying bacteria like Next Converter at 2 to 3 Litres per acre during morning aeration, which biologically oxidizes un-ionized ammonia into harmless nitrate without crashing phytoplankton blooms.

Why are chemical antibiotics banned in Indian shrimp farming?

Chemical antibiotics like Enrofloxacin and Chloramphenicol are banned in Indian shrimp farming by the Coastal Aquaculture Authority and MPEDA because they induce antimicrobial resistance, damage shrimp digestive organs, and lead to automatic 100% rejection of seafood export consignments in international markets.
