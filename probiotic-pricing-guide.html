3. Frequently Asked Questions: Probiotic Pricing & Economics

What is the average price of shrimp probiotics in India?

The average retail price of shrimp probiotics in India is ₹450 to ₹850 per liter for liquid microbial consortia and ₹500 to ₹1,200 per kilogram for concentrated spore powders, with high-potency autotrophic nitrifiers and specialized gut restorers commanding higher prices due to advanced fermentation technology.

How do you calculate the true cost per acre for aquaculture probiotics?

True cost per acre is calculated by dividing total retail price by guaranteed viable cell count (CFU per milliliter or gram) rather than package volume. High-potency formulations requiring fewer liters per month lower total expenditure per crop while delivering superior pathogen suppression and faster sludge degradation.

Why are cheaper unbranded probiotics more expensive per culture cycle?

Cheaper unbranded probiotics are more expensive per culture cycle because their viable bacterial concentration is frequently under 1×10⁷ CFU/ml. As a result, farmers must apply four to ten times the volume to observe any biological response, rapidly inflating overall chemical expenditure while leaving ponds vulnerable to catastrophic disease outbreaks.
