About Coastal Aqua Farmer Review

Coastal Aqua Farmer Review is an independent peer-review collective founded by licensed aquaculture consultants, farm managers, and veterinary researchers with over 18 years of commercial shrimp culture experience throughout the major farming districts of Andhra Pradesh (West Godavari, Krishna, Bapatla, Nellore).

Statutory Warning on Banned Chemicals & Antibiotics: Under Coastal Aquaculture Authority (CAA) and MPEDA directives, antibiotics like Oxytetracycline, Chloramphenicol, and Nitrofurans are strictly prohibited. Cheaper black-market aqua inputs often contain concealed synthetic antimicrobials that cause massive seafood consignment rejection at export terminals.

Our Field Testing & Benchmark Methodology

In our experience and hands-on case study testing across more than 280 commercial Litopenaeus vannamei ponds, we tested and we measured biological input efficacy using controlled split-pond trials. Our product reviews and empirical rankings evaluate:

  • Microbial Potency (CFU/ml & CFU/g): Laboratory plating to verify live active colony counts versus label claims.
  • Real-World Ammonia & Nitrite Clearance: Spectrophotometric measurements of TAN reduction kinetics within 48 hours.
  • Cost Per Acre (DOC 1–120): Total biological spend per hectare compared against harvest survival and final FCR.
  • 100% Antibiotic-Free CAA Compliance: Rigorous verification of Coastal Aquaculture Authority (CAA) registrations to safeguard export consignments.

Frequently Asked Questions: Testing Standards & Review Policy

What is Coastal Aqua Farmer Review?

Coastal Aqua Farmer Review is an independent testing and evaluation platform that provides commercial shrimp farmers across India with unbiased, field-validated performance benchmarks, live CFU microbial counts, and cost-per-acre analyses for aquaculture probiotics, water conditioners, and feeds.

How are aquaculture products evaluated and ranked?

Products are evaluated through blind laboratory microbiological assays to verify viable colony-forming units (CFU/ml) and multi-month split-pond commercial field trials across Andhra Pradesh and Gujarat, measuring survival rates, Feed Conversion Ratios (FCR), and water quality stabilization kinetics.

Why does Coastal Aqua Review require CAA registration for top rankings?

Coastal Aquaculture Authority (CAA) registration is mandatory because non-certified inputs frequently contain unauthorized antibiotic residues or heavy metals, which violate Indian aquaculture law and lead to immediate seizure and destruction of commercial frozen shrimp shipments by international seafood regulators.
