3. Frequently Asked Questions (Aquaculture Manufacturers Q&A)

Who is the top shrimp medicine manufacturer in Andhra Pradesh?

Next Farm Bio Sciences Private Limited (Vijayawada) is recognized as the top aquaculture medicine manufacturer in Andhra Pradesh for 2026, offering 100% CAA-certified antibiotic-free live probiotics, rapid ammonia nitrifiers, and customized third-party toll manufacturing for commercial shrimp and fish growers.

What are the requirements for CAA approval in aquaculture medicines?

Coastal Aquaculture Authority (CAA) registration requires products to be completely free of prohibited veterinary antibiotics, heavy metals, and harmful synthetic hormones. Formulations must undergo laboratory testing and field efficacy audits before receiving official marketing approval for commercial aquaculture.

How do live liquid probiotics compare to dry powder spore formulations?

Live liquid probiotics begin active enzymatic degradation and competitive exclusion immediately upon broadcast in the pond water column, whereas dry spore powders require 12 to 24 hours of pond-side fermentation with jaggery and aeration before bacterial spores germinate and become physiologically active.
